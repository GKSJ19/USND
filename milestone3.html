STEp-6 Top10 Disaster States (Bar chat)- Disaster Distribution.

In [37]:
# Get top 10 states
top_states = df['state'].value_counts().head(10).index

 
filtered_df = df[df['state'].isin(top_states)]

 
state_incident = filtered_df.groupby(['state','incidentType']).size().reset_index(name='count')

 
pivot_data = state_incident.pivot(index='state', columns='incidentType', values='count').fillna(0)

 
pivot_data.plot(kind='bar', stacked=True, figsize=(12,6))

plt.title("Incident Type Distribution in Top 10 Disaster States")
plt.xlabel("State")
plt.ylabel("Number of Disasters")

plt.xticks(rotation=90)
plt.legend(bbox_to_anchor=(1.05,1), loc='upper left')

plt.tight_layout()
plt.show()

The stacked bar chart shows the distribution of different disaster types across the top disaster-prone states. Certain states such as Texas and California experience multiple disaster types including storms, floods, and fires. Different geographic and climatic conditions in these states make them vulnerable to various natural disasters

Inference: Different states experience different types of disasters.
