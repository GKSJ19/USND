Cross-Milestone Synthesis

💡 Key Insights

Major findings synthesized from all 4 milestones of analysis

📈

Rising Disaster Frequency

Disaster declarations increased 5x from the 1950s to 2010s. The 2000s alone saw more declarations than the first 40 years combined, driven by improved reporting and climate factors.

⛈️

Storm Dominance

Storms account for 35%+ of all declarations, followed by floods (20%) and hurricanes (19%). These three categories represent 75% of all US disasters.

🗺️

Geographic Hotspots

Texas, California, Oklahoma, New York, and Florida are the top 5 most affected states. Gulf Coast and Tornado Alley face repeated seasonal devastation.

🍂

Seasonal Patterns

Spring (27%) and Fall (27%) are the busiest disaster seasons. Hurricane season (Jun–Nov) and tornado season (Mar–Jun) drive major spikes.

🏥

Assistance Programs

Public Assistance is activated in nearly all disasters, while Individual Assistance is heavily triggered by hurricanes, floods, and severe storms affecting residential areas.

🔄

Regional Recurring Patterns

States repeatedly face the same disaster types — Texas with storms, California with fires, Florida with hurricanes — highlighting the need for targeted preparedness.
